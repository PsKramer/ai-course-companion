Continuous batching

Watch for: with static batching, a group of 6 requests starts together and every slot waits (gray) until the longest one finishes. With continuous batching, a queued request joins the moment a slot frees up, after a one-step prefill (orange). Below, the paged key-value (KV) cache pool hands out 16-token blocks (2 MiB each for Course-8B) one at a time and frees them when a request ends. On the same GPU this gives 1.54× the tokens per second (Exercise 6.3.1). GIF version
