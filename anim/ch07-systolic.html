A systolic array, clock by clock

Watch for: a 4 × 4 weight-stationary systolic array. Each processing element (PE) holds one weight; activations enter from the left skewed by one clock per row and move right; partial sums move down and leave the bottom row. With M = 4 rows all 16 outputs finish after M + 2P − 2 = 10 clocks, and 64 of 160 PE-clocks are busy (40%). The replay with M = 1 takes 7 clocks at 14% utilization: short streams waste most of the array. GIF version
