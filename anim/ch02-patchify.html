From pixels to tokens

Watch for: a 448 × 448-pixel image is cut into 28 × 28 = 784 patches of 16 × 16 pixels; each patch flattens to 16 × 16 × 3 = 768 numbers; 2 × 2 merging leaves 196 image tokens, which a projector maps to Course-8B's model width d = 4,096. The image tokens join 5 text tokens in one 201-position sequence, and every position costs 128 KiB of key-value (KV) cache, so 201 positions take 25.1 MiB. Doubling the image side gives 4× the tokens (1024 × 1024 pixels → 1,024 tokens). GIF version
