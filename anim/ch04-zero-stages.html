What ZeRO shards

Watch for: the Zero Redundancy Optimizer (ZeRO) shards Course-8B's training state across an 8-GPU group. Plain distributed data parallel (DDP) keeps 129 GB per GPU (16 bytes per parameter), well over an H100's 80 GB. Stage 1 splits the 96 GB of fp32 master weights and Adam state (44 GB per GPU), stage 2 also splits the gradients (30 GB), and stage 3 / fully sharded data parallel (FSDP) splits the weights too (16 GB), but must all-gather each layer's weights just before that layer runs. Stages 1 and 2 cost no extra traffic; stage 3 sends 1.5× the gradient all-reduce. GIF version
