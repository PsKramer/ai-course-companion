One attention head

Watch for: how the token "it" gathers information. Every token's vector is multiplied by W_Q, W_K and W_V to give a query, key and value. The query of "it" is dot-multiplied with the keys of all tokens up to itself (later tokens are masked), divided by √128 (head dimension 128) and passed through softmax, giving weights that sum to 1 (illustrative scores; "cat" gets 0.60). The output is the weighted sum of the value vectors. Every token does the same, giving a lower-triangular grid. Course-8B caches keys and values: 131,072 bytes (128 KiB) per token. GIF version
