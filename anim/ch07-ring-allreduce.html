Ring all-reduce

Watch for: four GPUs, each holding a buffer split into 4 chunks (GPU r starts with the value r + 1), sum their buffers around a ring. Reduce-scatter (3 steps): every GPU sends one chunk to its right neighbour, which adds it; afterwards each GPU owns one fully summed chunk (10). All-gather (3 steps): the finished chunks circulate until every GPU holds 10 everywhere. Each GPU sends 2(n − 1)/n · S = 1.5 S bytes; time = 2(n − 1)α + 2(n − 1)/n · S/β, with α the latency per step and β the link bandwidth. GIF version
