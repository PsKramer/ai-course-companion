Pipeline bubbles: GPipe vs 1F1B

Watch for: 4 pipeline stages and 8 micro-batches, with each backward pass taking twice as long as a forward pass. GPipe runs all forwards and then all backwards, so stage 0 holds activations for all 8 micro-batches. One-forward-one-backward (1F1B) alternates after a warmup and holds at most 4 (p). The idle time is the same in both: (p − 1)/(m + p − 1) = 3/11 = 27%. Only more micro-batches (m = 32 gives 9%) or interleaving shrink it. GIF version
