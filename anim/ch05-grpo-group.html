One GRPO step

Watch for: one step of group relative policy optimization (GRPO). The policy samples 8 answers to "17 × 24 = ?"; a verifier gives reward 1 to the 3 correct ones and 0 to the 5 wrong ones. The group mean (0.375) is the baseline and the standard deviation (0.484) sets the scale, so each answer's advantage is (r − mean)/std: +1.29 for correct answers and −0.77 for wrong ones, applied to every token of the answer. A group that is all correct has advantage 0 everywhere and teaches nothing; DAPO (decoupled clip and dynamic sampling policy optimization) resamples such prompts. No value model is needed. GIF version
