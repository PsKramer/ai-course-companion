The residual stream

Watch for: one token's journey through Course-8B. The token ID picks a row of the 128,256 × 4096 embedding table, giving the vector h (4,096 numbers). Each of the 64 sublayers (attention and feed-forward network, FFN, in each of 32 layers) reads a copy normalized by root-mean-square normalization (RMSNorm) and adds its output back: h ← h + f(RMSNorm(h)). The stream is never overwritten, so its size grows slowly with depth (the meter is illustrative). A final RMSNorm and the 4096 × 128,256 unembedding turn h into next-token probabilities. GIF version
