What to notice

Context × concurrency is conserved per GPU: the budget holds a fixed number of tokens, whether that's 52 users at 8,192 tokens or 3 users at 131,072. Past about 122,500 tokens in flight for Course-8B, KV bytes exceed weight bytes in every decode step, so shrinking the cache (GQA, MLA, FP8 KV) buys more than shrinking the weights.
Model (as in 6.2). skv = 2·L·hkv·dh·b (keys and values, every layer). MLA-style caches one latent per layer: L·(dc + dR)·b. Sliding-window hybrid, averaged over the context: (2·hkv·dh·b / T)·(Lg·T + Lloc·min(T, W)), where Lg = global layers and Lloc = L − Lg local layers. Budget = HBM − N·bw − 10% of HBM for activations and fragmentation. Decode step time = (weights + users·T·skv) / bandwidth, the memory-bound model from 1.3.
