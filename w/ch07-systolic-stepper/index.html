What to notice

Each weight is fetched once and used by every row that passes; each activation is used by every PE in its row, all without going back to memory. But the array takes P − 1 clocks to fill and P − 1 to drain, so the busy PEs form a diagonal wavefront and short streams (small M, such as batch-1 decode) leave most of the array idle.
Model (as in 7.7 and Exercise 7.7.1). Weight-stationary: weight W[k,n] sits in PE (k, n); activation X[m,k] reaches PE (k, n) at clock m + k + n (0-indexed), moving right; the partial sum of Y[m,n] moves down column n and leaves the bottom at clock m + n + P − 1. Total M + 2P − 2 clocks; utilization M/(M + 2P − 2). Output-stationary: PE (r, n) keeps the running sum of Y[m,n] for one P-row tile; X[m,k] enters from the left and W[k,n] from the top, meeting at clock k + r + n; each tile takes 3P − 2 clocks here (tiles not overlapped, results read out at the end). Small integers so the sums can be checked by hand.
