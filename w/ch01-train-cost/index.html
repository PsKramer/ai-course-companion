What to notice

Cost is linear in both N and D, so overtraining a small model (Course-8B sees ~1,900 tokens/param, ~90× Chinchilla) is cheap next to training a bigger one, and pays back at inference where cost scales with N. For 405B, 6ND gives ≈3.8×10²⁵ FLOP and ≈27M GPU-hours at 40% MFU vs 30.84M reported: the gap is real-world MFU (Meta reports 38–43%), attention FLOPs, and failures/restarts. MFU is a direct multiplier on the bill.
Model. C ≈ 6·N·D (2ND forward + 4ND backward per token; dense model, for MoE use N_act). The attention option adds Kaplan et al. (2020)'s per-token term 2·L·T·d forward, ×3 for forward+backward, with L and d estimated from N ≈ 12·L·d² at d/L = 128. GPU-hours = C / (peak · MFU · 3600). Cost is GPU rental only; ablations, failed runs, data, and staff push real budgets several times higher. Reference values: Epoch AI notable-models database (est. = Epoch estimate; lab has not published).
