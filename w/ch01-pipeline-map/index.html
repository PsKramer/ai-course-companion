What to notice

Every boundary is a typed artifact with a computable size (token IDs [B, T], weights N·bytes, KV cache per token), so a defect upstream is paid for in every later stage. Architecture fixes the KV-cache size; serving pays for it. Hardware constrains every stage, not only training: training is a one-time FLOP bill (≈ 6·N·D), serving a recurring bandwidth and memory-capacity bill.
Numbers are order-of-magnitude anchors for 2024–2026 frontier and open models (e.g. Llama 3.1 405B: 15.6T training tokens, 3.8×10²⁵ FLOP); the chapter pages carry exact sources.
