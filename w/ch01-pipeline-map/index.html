What to notice

Every stage's output is the next stage's input, so a defect upstream (bad dedup, a tokenizer that splits numbers badly) is paid for in every later stage. Training is a one-time FLOP bill dominated by 6·N·D; serving is a recurring bill dominated by memory bandwidth and KV-cache capacity, which is why the hardware story differs between the two.
Numbers are order-of-magnitude anchors for 2024–2026 frontier and open models (e.g. Llama 3.1 405B: 15.6T training tokens, 3.8×10²⁵ FLOP); the chapter pages carry exact sources.
